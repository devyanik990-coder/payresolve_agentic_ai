What is PayResolve AI?

PayResolve AI is an AI-Powered payment operations investigation platform designed to help Payment Operations and Risk Analysts investigate transaction authorization failures and potential fraud through structured, evidence-based investigations.
The platform transforms manual payment investigation into a structured workflow where specialized AI agents analyze failure patterns, identify likely root causes, assess revenue impact, and recommend recovery actions while keeping human analysts in control of final decisions.


Key Capabilities:

Investigation Queue - Prioritize payment investigation cases based on business impact and urgency.
Transaction Investigation - Group related failed transactions into structured investigation cases.
AI-Powered Analysis - Use specialized AI agents to classify failures and identify likely root causes.
evidence-based Findings - Provide supporting evidence behind investigation conclusions.
Revenue Impact Analysis - Assess the potential business impact of payment authorization failures.
Recovery Recommendations - Generate actionable recommendations based on investigation findings.
human-in-the-loop Review - Allow analysts to review, approve, override, or escalate AI-generated findings.
Auditability - Maintain traceable investigation findings, evidence, recommendations, and decisions.


How It Works?

Investigation Queue -> Investigation Case -> AI-Powered Investigation -> Classification & Root Cause -> Evidence & Revenue Impact -> Recovery Recommendations -> Human Review -> Approve / Override / Escalate -> Resolution


Why Agentic AI?

PayResolve AI uses a structured, multi-agent investigation approach rather than relying on a single AI interaction. Specialized agents contribute to different stages of the investigation, including failure classification, root-cause analysis, revenue impact assessment, recovery recommendations, and reporting.
The product follows a human-in-the-loop model: AI supports investigation and reasoning, while the analyst remains responsible for reviewing and making the final decision.
